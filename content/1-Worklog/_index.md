**On this page**, you will introduce your worklog by describing how your internship was organized, **how many weeks** you spent completing the program, and **what activities and tasks** you performed during each week.

**Week 1:** [FCJ Orientation & AWS Fundamentals (21/09/2026 – 26/09/2026)](1.1-week1/)

**Week 4:** [Snaptics Project Discussion, Sitemap, Wireframe & AWS Services (12/10/2026 – 16/10/2026)](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [SPA Frontend Setup, Design System Tokens, Layout & Authentication Interfaces (19/10/2026 – 23/10/2026)](1.5-week5/)
