## I. General Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

I highly appreciate the working environment at FCAJ for its professionalism, friendliness, and openness. From the very first days of the program, I received a warm welcome from the mentors, admin team, and other members. Everyone creates a supportive atmosphere where interns feel comfortable exchanging ideas, asking questions, and sharing difficulties encountered during their learning and working processes.

Whenever I encounter technical issues or struggle to understand a new concept, I can always receive timely support. What impresses me most is that the mentors do not simply answer questions but also proactively follow up on individual progress and show genuine interest in each member's development. This makes me feel supported and motivates me to take initiative, continuously improve myself, and complete assigned tasks responsibly.

In addition, the office environment is clean, organized, and comfortable, creating favorable conditions for maintaining focus and productivity. Overall, the combination of a professional working environment, an open culture, and a supportive community has made my internship experience very positive and meaningful.

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team

I am genuinely grateful for the guidance and support provided by the mentors throughout the program. They always take the time to explain technical concepts clearly and relate them to practical situations, helping interns understand not only the underlying concepts but also how to apply them in real-world projects.

One thing I particularly appreciate is that the mentors do not simply provide ready-made solutions. Instead, they encourage me to conduct my own research, analyze problems, and experiment with different approaches. This guidance has helped me develop problem-solving skills, improve my ability to learn independently, and become more confident when facing new technical challenges.

The admin team also plays an important role in organizing and maintaining the program. Important announcements regarding schedules, learning materials, deadlines, and activities are communicated clearly and promptly. Thanks to their careful preparation and coordination, I can manage my time effectively and focus more on learning and completing assigned tasks.

I truly appreciate the dedication of both the mentors and the admin team, as their support has made the internship experience smoother, more productive, and more valuable.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Major

I believe that the FCAJ program is highly relevant to my Information Technology major and future career goals. It provides an excellent opportunity to transform the knowledge I have acquired at university into practical skills through workshops, technical assignments, and project development.

During the program, I had the opportunity to explore Cloud Computing more deeply, particularly the AWS ecosystem. Rather than simply learning about individual services, I also gained a better understanding of how different services work together to build, deploy, and operate a complete system.

Through these experiences, I realized that software development involves much more than writing code. It also requires knowledge of system architecture, security, data management, performance optimization, monitoring, and scalability.

Furthermore, working on team projects has helped me improve important professional skills, including task allocation, progress management, technical discussions, and collaborative problem-solving. I have also learned to consider different perspectives, communicate my ideas clearly, and take responsibility for my assigned tasks.

These experiences have strengthened my academic knowledge and helped me define my future career direction more clearly, particularly in Cloud Computing, AWS, and the practical application of Artificial Intelligence in software products.

### 4. Company Culture and Teamwork

One of the things I appreciate most about FCAJ is its working culture, which emphasizes collaboration, initiative, and collective responsibility. Although each member has individual responsibilities, everyone is willing to support one another and contribute toward shared goals rather than focusing solely on personal tasks.

During teamwork activities, I have had opportunities to exchange ideas and learn about different approaches from other members. When challenges arise, everyone works together to discuss possible solutions, share experiences, and learn from mistakes instead of focusing on assigning blame to individuals.

Through these experiences, I have realized that effective teamwork requires regular communication, clear responsibilities, proactive progress updates, and mutual respect. More importantly, every team member should understand that individual performance directly contributes to the overall success of the team.

Even as an intern, I have always felt that my opinions and contributions are respected. This has encouraged me to become more confident, proactive, and responsible when carrying out my assigned tasks.

### 5. Intern Policies and Benefits

I highly appreciate the learning and development opportunities that FCAJ provides for interns, especially the opportunity to participate in professional events, technology seminars, and knowledge-sharing sessions with experienced professionals in the industry. These activities not only help interns broaden their knowledge but also provide valuable exposure to the real-world technology environment and a better understanding of industry expectations.

One of my memorable experiences was attending **Cloud Mastery 1**, held on **November 14, 2026**, on the 26th floor of Bitexco Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. This event was a meaningful opportunity for FPT students interested in Cloud Computing to exchange knowledge, listen to practical insights, and learn from the experiences of professionals working in the technology industry.

Through this event, I had the opportunity to strengthen my fundamental knowledge of Cloud Computing and gain a better understanding of its role in modern application development. I also recognized the importance of combining theoretical knowledge with practical experience. The speakers' insights provided me with broader perspectives on continuous learning, professional development, and career opportunities in the Information Technology industry.

What impressed me most was that the event focused not only on technical knowledge but also encouraged active learning, communication, and networking among students who share similar interests. I attended the event with a teammate, which gave us an opportunity to learn together, exchange useful insights, and discuss how the knowledge gained could be applied to our **Snaptics** project.

After attending the event, I realized that I should continue exploring Cloud platforms, particularly the AWS ecosystem, while paying more attention to important aspects of system architecture such as scalability, reliability, availability, and operational efficiency. These insights can be valuable when improving the architecture and planning the future development of our product.

Overall, I truly appreciate the opportunities FCAJ provides through extracurricular activities and professional events. Experiences like Cloud Mastery 1 have helped me not only strengthen my technical knowledge but also improve my communication skills, develop a continuous learning mindset, and build connections within the technology community. I hope the program will continue maintaining and expanding similar activities so that future interns can gain more practical knowledge, stay updated with emerging technology trends, and develop both professionally and personally.

## II. Additional Questions

### 1. What Are You Most Satisfied With During Your Internship?

What satisfies me most is the opportunity to directly participate in designing, developing, and deploying a practical Cloud-Native product called **Snaptics – An AI-Powered Smart Expense Management and Receipt Scanning Platform**.

Instead of only learning about AWS services through theoretical materials, I had the opportunity to work with several core AWS services, including Amazon S3, Amazon SQS, Amazon ECS Fargate, AWS Amplify, Amazon CloudFront, and Amazon CloudWatch. I also explored how AI/OCR solutions can be integrated into data-processing workflows.

What I gained was not limited to understanding individual services. I also developed a better perspective on how different components interact to form a complete system. Throughout the development process, I had opportunities to face practical challenges, analyze problems, experiment with different approaches, and improve solutions.

In particular, the guidance and feedback from mentors helped me better understand system design principles and how to approach a technology project in a real-world development environment. This has been the most valuable experience for me and something I am genuinely proud of when reflecting on my internship.

### 2. What Do You Think the Company Should Improve for Future Interns?

I believe the program has already provided a solid foundation for learning and practical experience. However, it would be even better if FCAJ could organize more technical workshops and in-depth sessions focusing on advanced topics in the AWS and AI ecosystems.

Some topics that I believe would be particularly valuable include Serverless Architecture, Event-Driven Architecture, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, DevSecOps, Infrastructure as Code, and AWS cost optimization strategies.

In addition to introducing technical concepts, these sessions could incorporate small hands-on exercises or real-world scenarios, allowing interns to apply what they have learned directly. This would help participants gain a deeper understanding of solution selection, evaluating technical trade-offs, and handling common challenges during system deployment.

I believe these activities would make the program even more practical, encourage continuous learning, and help interns become better prepared for real-world industry requirements.

### 3. Would You Recommend This Internship Program to Your Friends? Why?

Absolutely. I would be more than happy to recommend FCAJ to friends who are looking for opportunities to develop their skills in the technology industry, particularly in Cloud Computing and AWS.

What I appreciate most about the program is its combination of foundational knowledge, technical workshops, direct mentorship, and opportunities to participate in developing practical products. Interns are not only introduced to new technologies but also encouraged to develop problem-solving abilities, teamwork skills, and a proactive working mindset.

In particular, the Snaptics project gave me the opportunity to experience a practical Cloud-Native product development process, helping me better understand the knowledge and skills required to work in the technology industry.

I believe FCAJ is an excellent environment for young people to learn, challenge themselves, and gradually establish their career paths in the technology field.

## III. Suggestions and Expectations

### 1. Do You Have Any Suggestions to Improve the Internship Experience?

One suggestion I sincerely hope FCAJ will consider for future programs is providing more opportunities for interns to work directly at the office on a regular basis.

Although online learning and remote working offer flexibility, I believe face-to-face interactions can bring significant benefits to communication and collaboration. Interns would be able to discuss technical problems with mentors more easily, receive feedback more quickly, exchange ideas directly, and gain a better understanding of how a professional team operates in a corporate environment.

Additionally, the program could combine on-site working sessions with project progress reviews, technical sharing sessions, hands-on workshops, and experience-sharing activities between mentors and interns. These activities would provide opportunities not only to strengthen technical knowledge but also to improve communication, teamwork, and professional working skills.

I believe that combining online learning with more frequent on-site experiences would create a more comprehensive internship program and provide interns with a closer experience of the actual working environment.

### 2. Would You Like to Continue Participating in This Program in the Future?

Yes. I would genuinely love to have the opportunity to continue being part of FCAJ in the future, especially by participating in more practical activities and having additional opportunities to work directly at the office.

I would like to continue developing and improving the Snaptics project while strengthening my knowledge of the AWS ecosystem and exploring Cloud-Native Architecture, Serverless technologies, AI, and Agentic AI in greater depth.

Beyond technical development, I also hope to continue learning from mentors, collaborating with people who share similar interests in technology, and challenging myself with more complex tasks.

I consider FCAJ an environment with great potential for my continued growth, where I can gain valuable experience and better prepare myself for my future career after graduation.

### 3. Any Other Comments?

First of all, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the entire FCAJ team, the mentors, and the admin team for their dedication, enthusiasm, and continuous support throughout the program.

For me, this internship has been more than just an opportunity to explore new technologies or complete a technical project. It has also been a valuable period of personal growth that has helped me improve my mindset, working habits, and career direction.

I particularly appreciate the practical knowledge shared throughout the program, the guidance from mentors, the opportunities to participate in technology events, and the experience of working in a professional environment.

I hope FCAJ will continue to grow and expand its learning activities, advanced technical workshops, and opportunities for interns to work directly at the office. I also hope that future generations of interns will have the opportunity to experience the same supportive learning environment, challenge themselves with practical projects, and develop their abilities just as I have throughout this journey.

**Once again, I would like to sincerely thank FCAJ for providing me with such a memorable and meaningful internship!**
