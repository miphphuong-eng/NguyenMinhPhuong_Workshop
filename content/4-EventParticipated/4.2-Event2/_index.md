# Summary Report: "Cloud Mastery 1"

### Event Objectives

- Provide FPT students with an opportunity to learn more about **Cloud Computing** and its applications.
- Create an environment for students to exchange knowledge and learn from the experiences of people with more experience in the technology industry.
- Help students gain a better understanding of cloud technologies and their importance in modern software development.
- Encourage students to actively participate in university activities and connect with the technology community.
- Inspire students to continue developing their technical knowledge and practical skills.

### Event Information

- **Event Name:** Cloud Mastery 1.
- **Date:** November 14, 2026 (Saturday).
- **Venue:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Registration:** Direct registration through the university portal.
- **Format:** On-site knowledge-sharing event.

### Key Highlights

#### Cloud Computing Knowledge Sharing

- **Cloud fundamentals:** Gain a broader understanding of Cloud Computing and its role in modern technology.
- **Industry knowledge:** Learn from the experiences and knowledge shared by more experienced professionals.
- **Practical perspectives:** Understand how cloud technologies are connected to real-world software development.

#### Learning from Experienced Professionals

- **Experience sharing:** Listen to senior professionals share the knowledge and experience they have gained throughout their learning and working journeys.
- **Technical development:** Understand the importance of continuously improving technical knowledge and practical skills.
- **Career insights:** Gain useful perspectives on learning and developing a career in the technology industry.

#### Student Engagement and Networking

- **Community connection:** Meet and interact with other FPT students who are interested in Cloud Computing.
- **Team participation:** Participate in the event with a teammate to learn and explore cloud technologies together.
- **Knowledge exchange:** Share learning interests and perspectives with other participants.

### Key Takeaways

#### Cloud Computing

- **Cloud awareness:** Develop a better understanding of the role of cloud technologies in modern applications.
- **Practical learning:** Recognize the importance of combining theoretical knowledge with practical experience.
- **Continuous exploration:** Stay curious and continue learning about cloud platforms, services, and tools.

#### Learning from Experience

- **Knowledge sharing:** Learn from the experiences and advice shared by people with more experience.
- **Professional development:** Understand that technical growth requires continuous learning and practice.
- **Learning mindset:** Be proactive in exploring new technologies and improving personal skills.

#### Collaboration and Communication

- **Teamwork:** Recognize the value of learning and exploring new technologies with teammates.
- **Communication:** Improve the ability to exchange ideas and discuss technical topics.
- **Networking:** Build connections with other students who share similar interests in technology.

### Applying to Work

- **Improve cloud knowledge:** Apply the knowledge gained to the development of the Smart Expense Tracker project.
- **Explore AWS services:** Continue learning about AWS services that can support application development and infrastructure management.
- **Improve system architecture:** Consider scalability, reliability, and availability when designing application infrastructure.
- **Share knowledge with teammates:** Discuss useful insights from the event and apply relevant knowledge to the project.
- **Continue practical learning:** Explore cloud technologies through hands-on practice and real-world projects.

### Event Experience

Participating in **Cloud Mastery 1** was a valuable opportunity to learn more about Cloud Computing, listen to experience-sharing sessions, and connect with other FPT students interested in technology.

#### Learning from Experienced Professionals

- Listened to experienced professionals share the knowledge and lessons they had gained throughout their learning and working journeys.
- Gained additional perspectives on the importance of technical knowledge and practical experience.
- Recognized the value of learning from people who have more experience in the technology industry.

#### Understanding the Importance of Cloud Computing

- Gained a better understanding of why Cloud Computing is an important technology in modern software development.
- Became more interested in exploring cloud platforms and their practical applications.
- Recognized the importance of continuously updating technical knowledge to keep up with industry developments.

#### Participating with a Teammate

- Attended the event with a teammate to learn more about Cloud Computing together.
- Shared observations and discussed useful information from the event.
- Had an opportunity to connect with other students and expand our understanding of cloud technologies.

#### Lessons Learned

- Learning from experienced professionals provides valuable perspectives beyond classroom knowledge.
- Cloud Computing is an important area of technology that requires continuous learning and practical exploration.
- Sharing knowledge and experiences helps students develop a broader understanding of the industry.
- Learning together and exchanging ideas with teammates can make the learning process more effective.
