# Summary Report: "Smart Plushies for Children"

### 1. Event Objectives

The Smart Plushies for Children event was a volunteer activity organized in collaboration with **Operation Smile Vietnam**. The event aimed to combine creativity, basic technology, and community support through a hands-on activity.

The main objectives were:

- Participate in a meaningful volunteer activity to support children through Operation Smile Vietnam.
- Learn how to create a handmade plush toy that can move using the materials and instructions provided at the event.
- Explore how simple technical elements can be incorporated into everyday objects to make them more interactive.
- Develop creativity, patience, and practical problem-solving skills through a hands-on activity.
- Contribute a handmade gift that could bring happiness and encouragement to children.

### 2. Event Information

- **Event Name:** Smart Plushies for Children
- **Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Time:** 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM
- **Location:** EC2, 36th Floor, HCMC Office
- **Organization:** Operation Smile Vietnam
- **Role:** Attendee and Volunteer

### 3. Event Activities and Highlights

**3.1. Introduction to the Volunteer Activity**

At the beginning of the event, participants were introduced to the purpose of the Smart Plushies for Children initiative and its collaboration with Operation Smile Vietnam.

The activity focused on creating moving plush toys that would later be given to children supported by the organization. I found this purpose especially meaningful because participants were not simply making a toy for themselves but creating something that could become a special gift for someone else.

One thing I appreciated was that no previous programming or technical experience was required. All necessary materials and instructions were provided, allowing participants from different backgrounds to take part.

**3.2. Creating a Moving Plush Toy**

The main activity was making a plush toy that could move. I had the opportunity to work directly with the provided materials and follow the instructions to create my own product.

This was an interesting experience because it allowed me to learn through practice rather than only listening to explanations. I needed to pay attention to each step, carefully handle the materials, and make sure the different parts were put together properly.

Through this activity, I also gained a better appreciation of how creativity and simple technical ideas can be combined to turn an ordinary plush toy into something more interactive.

**3.3. Contributing to a Charitable Cause**

The most meaningful part of the event was knowing that the plush toy I created would be given to children supported by Operation Smile Vietnam.

Although making one plush toy might seem like a small contribution, it represented the time, effort, and care that I put into creating something for others. I hoped that when the children received these handmade toys, they would feel happy and encouraged.

This activity helped me understand that volunteering does not always require a large contribution. Even a small action, when done with care and good intentions, can bring positive value to the community.

### 4. Skills and Knowledge Gained

Through this event, I gained several useful experiences:

- **Practical Skills:** Improved my ability to follow instructions and complete a hands-on activity using the provided materials.
- **Creativity:** Learned to approach a simple handmade product from a more creative and interactive perspective.
- **Patience and Attention to Detail:** Understood the importance of carefully completing each step to achieve the intended result.
- **Technology Awareness:** Gained a better understanding of how basic technical elements can be applied in everyday products.
- **Social Responsibility:** Recognized the value of using personal time and effort to contribute to meaningful community activities.

### 5. Personal Experience and Reflection

I really enjoyed participating in Smart Plushies for Children because it was different from my usual technical learning activities. As an IT student, I normally spend much of my time learning about programming, software development, and technical concepts. This event gave me an opportunity to step outside that routine and create something with my own hands.

At first, I was curious about how a normal plush toy could be transformed into a toy that could move. During the activity, I learned by following the instructions and working directly with the materials. It was satisfying to see the product gradually take shape.

What made the experience even more special was its charitable purpose. I felt happy and proud knowing that the plush toy I made was intended to be given directly to children supported by Operation Smile Vietnam. I hope that my small contribution can bring a little happiness to the children who receive it.

The event also reminded me that technology and creativity are not only about building applications or solving technical problems. They can also be used in simple ways to create meaningful experiences and make a positive difference in people's lives.

### 6. Conclusion

Overall, Smart Plushies for Children was a meaningful experience that combined hands-on learning with community service. I had the opportunity to explore a creative activity, develop practical skills, and contribute to a charitable initiative.

More importantly, this event helped me realize that the value of an activity is not measured only by the product created, but also by the purpose behind it. I am grateful to have participated and hope to join more activities that allow me to learn new things while contributing positively to the community.
